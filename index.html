Specifications

Every detail counted.

DAC Architecture
Dual ESS Sabre ES9038Q2M Chips

Independent balanced mono-mode converters for left and right channels, delivering 32-bit/768kHz PCM and native DSD512 decoding.

Audio Outputs
3.5mm Stereo + 4.4mm Pentaconn Balanced

Output power: 240mW @ 32Ω (3.5mm) / 580mW @ 32Ω (4.4mm balanced). Drives high-impedance studio planar headphones up to 600Ω.

Frequency & Distortion
5Hz – 90,000Hz (±0.05dB)

Total Harmonic Distortion (THD+N): 0.0003%. Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR): 128dB. Dynamic range: 130dB.

Storage & Memory
256GB Internal + Up to 2TB MicroSD

Holds approximately 40,000 CD-quality FLAC tracks or 5,000 high-resolution 24-bit/192kHz studio master albums.

Battery & Power
Up to 40 Hours Continuous Lossless Playback

3,200mAh high-density lithium-polymer cell. USB-C Power Delivery fast charging: 80% charge in 45 minutes.

Supported Formats
All Major Lossless and Lossy Formats

FLAC, WAV, ALAC, AIFF, DSD (DSF/DFF), MQA 8x, APE, OGG, AAC, MP3, WMA. Two-way USB-C DAC mode for Mac and PC.
